About Us

Humane Trapping, Not Poison

My name is Dave McCoy. I am a birdwatcher, angler and falconer, and I have spent a lot of my life out in the countryside. That is why I do not rely on rodenticides: poisoned rodents crawl away and are eaten by owls, kestrels and other birds of prey, and the poison goes straight up the food chain.

Trained to British Pest Control Association standards. Humane trapping that is child and pet safe, and the trapped rodents are taken away rather than left to decompose in your walls.

Why Choose D M Pest Control Shrewsbury

  • Trained to BPCA standardsProfessionally trained to British Pest Control Association standards, and working to them on every job.
  • Child and pet safeHumane trapping removes the risk of secondary poisoning to pets and non target wildlife that comes with rodenticides.
  • No smell in the cavitiesPoisoned rodents die where you cannot reach them. Trapped ones get taken away, so there is nothing left to decompose.
  • Advice so you do not call againI explain what let them in and what to change, because a repeat callout is a job I would rather you did not need.
